## 🔍 Why These Scores?

### The 0/10 Scores Explained:

#### 1. **Trust & Social Proof: 0/10**

**Why this happened:**
- Netflix homepage is VERY minimal
- No traditional "testimonials"
- Social proof is built into the product (shows, ratings)
- Our patterns look for explicit testimonials

**Is this a problem?**
- For Netflix: NO - their brand IS the social proof
- For most companies: YES - you need testimonials

**The tool is technically correct but misses context**

#### 2. **Tracking: 0/10**

**Why this happened:**
- Netflix likely uses proprietary tracking
- Heavy JavaScript rendering
- Anti-scraping measures
- Privacy-focused implementation

**Reality:**
- Netflix definitely has tracking (they're data-driven!)
- Our detector just can't find it

**Action needed:** Selenium might help, but Netflix is tough to scrape
